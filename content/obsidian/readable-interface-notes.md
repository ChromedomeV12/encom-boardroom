High-contrast cyan text on black can look sharp in screenshots and still become tiring in a long article.

## Practical Defaults

- Body copy uses off-white text without glow.
- Cyan and yellow stay in borders, labels, links, and small status elements.
- Motion respects reduced-motion preferences.
- Panels collapse into a normal reading stack on small screens.

> [!warning] Contrast check
> The theme should be tested on a real laptop display, not only in a design mockup.
